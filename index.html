03 — Post-op recovery

Your recovery, week by week

Tap a week to see what we do and why.

Week 1

Swelling peaks

We start drainage from day 3 with your surgeon’s OK. Gentle, never painful, and we check your garment fit.

  • 2–3 drainage sessions
  • Garment and foam check
  • Daily check-in text
Weeks 2–3

Hard spots appear

Fibrosis can start to form. We add ultrasound to keep tissue soft and smooth.

  • 2 sessions a week
  • Ultrasound for firm areas
  • Photos and measurements
Weeks 4–6

Shape shows

Swelling drops and your new shape appears. We taper sessions and teach you self-massage.

  • 1 session a week
  • Self-massage coaching
  • Garment step-down plan
Week 6+

Refine & tighten

Optional RF tightening or cavitation for loose skin and leftover pockets.

  • RF skin tightening
  • Cavitation for small areas
  • Final photo comparison
